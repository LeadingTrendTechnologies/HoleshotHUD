# Strategy

Operate · F8 Profile → Strategy. Same night-ink settings as Motos. Not a HUD widget.

## Audience / job
Rider in the garage, before they set the tank. They just practiced, or they remember the last practice on this bike.

## Direction
Existing settings world. Charcoal rows, orange chip for the selected race type, one large orange fill.

## Memorable moment
The liters to type in the garage. Everything else explains that number.

## Layout
- Title Strategy, one line on what the page is for.
- Track, class, and setup on one line.
- Empty: no full laps yet, or no track yet.
- Rows: median, best, spread, liters per lap, liters per minute.
- Chips: Laps or Minutes. Stepper for the count. Note if it came from the last race here.
- Orange line: Put N L in the tank. Garage note under it. Spare lap or 10%. Dry lap or minute when the tank is short. Projected time on a lap race.

## Constraints
- Does not write Motos and does not move the garage slider.
- Fuel stays liters in the store. The row uses the liquids unit already chosen in Look.
