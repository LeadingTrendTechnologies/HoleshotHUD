Holeshot HUD — a free, fully customizable overlay for MX Bikes
MX Bikes does not give you much on-screen. Holeshot HUD sits on top of the game and shows live race data the way *you* want it: standings, gaps, a dash, maps, and who’s around you.
It is fully customizable. Nothing is locked in. Every widget can be turned on or off, moved, resized, restyled, and filled with the data you actually use. Build a tiny race HUD, a full broadcast layout, or anything in between.
Try the widgets in the browser first (demo data), then download for Windows:
https://holeshot-hud.vercel.app/
This is still a work in progress. If something’s off, or you have an idea, there’s a Feedback tab in the app (F8 → Feedback) to report bugs, leave a rating, and send feature ideas. I read those and use them to decide what to fix and build next.

🏁 Why use it

  • See the race, not just the bike. Place, gap (time or laps), interval, best/last lap, who is ahead and behind you, and where everyone is on the track.
  • Passes update live. Order on the boards follows the track, so you don’t wait on the game classification to catch up.
  • White / checkered flags on the dash or as their own Flags widget, plus a `~Lapped` tag when you’re a lap down.
  • Proximity. Radar with range rings so close vs far is a glance; Relative for the battle you’re actually in.
  • Free. No account. No admin install. Setup finds Steam MX Bikes and drops the plugin in.
  • Try before you install. The site has the same widgets as the app. Turn things on, drag them, change columns and fonts, then download if you like it. Holeshot HUD — live widget editor
  • Fits how you play. Launch with MX Bikes, close with the game, remap the settings key if Medal already uses F8.
  • Updated constantly. New builds land often. Enable **Update automatically on launch** in Settings (recommended) so you pick them up when the overlay starts.

⚙️ Fully customizable

A fresh install starts **blank** — every widget is hidden until you turn on **Show on overlay**. You only put on screen what you want.
Then make it yours:
  • Layout — Hold **Ctrl** and drag to move or resize. Snap any widget to a corner, edge, or center of the monitor.
  • Look — Font family (Exo 2, Teko, Goldman, Montserrat, Segoe, Arial, Tahoma, Roboto), per-widget font size, bold, and panel opacity.
  • Units — Metric or imperial for the whole HUD.
  • Columns — Standings and Relative: turn columns on/off, drag to reorder, slide widths. Position, number, name, gap, interval, laps, best/last, bike, status, penalty, and more.
  • Headers and footers — Three slots each on Standings / Relative (session time, riders, track, fuel, fuel %, etc.). Dash footer is three slots you pick (temps, laps, gap, bike, class, fuel, …). Optional rev bar.
  • Maps — Toggle other riders, start/finish, sector lines (S1 at the line), direction arrows, leader crown, nearest ahead/behind, and numbers or position in the dots.
  • Horizontal Standings — Broadcast-style bar: how many riders, track name, autoscroll, side info.
  • Radar — Side and/or rear proximity, with optional 3 / 6 / 12 m range rings.
Press F8 for settings (or pick another key). Layout is saved. Want a different look next session? Change it in a few clicks — you are not stuck with a default HUD.

🧩 Widgets

  • Standings — Who is ahead and by how much. Place, number, name, gap (time or laps), best/last lap, fuel, and more. Your row is highlighted. In replay, click a name to follow that rider.
  • Relative — Riders just ahead and behind you, so you can see the battle you’re actually in without scanning the full board. Fuel can sit in the header or footer.
  • Horizontal Standings — Broadcast-style bar: leader on the left, your name highlighted. Optional track name, autoscroll, and fuel in a side slot. In replay, click a card to follow that rider.
  • Map — Full track outline with you and other riders, start/finish, optional S1/S2/S3 sector lines (S1 at the line), direction arrows, leader crown, and optional numbers or position in the dots. Blue if they’re a lap ahead and closing, red if you’re a lap up and closing.
  • Minimap — Zoomed circular view of the track around you, same rider markers and sector lines.
  • Radar — Who is beside and behind you, with optional range rings so close vs far is a glance.
  • Dash — Gear, speed, RPM, position, and a footer you can set (including fuel). Optional rev bar, or Simple dash (gear and speed only). Session clock and race flags (white / checkered) sit with the dash.
  • Lean — Bike roll, pitch, and steering for the rider the camera is on. Figure (rear-view rider) or Minimal (numbers). Not sit / stand.
  • Delta Bar — Time vs your best at this point on the lap, per class on that track. Not the in-game ghost.
  • Sectors — Split times (S1–S3) and live delta vs your best at this point in the sector. Best times are per class on that track (all 250s together, all 450s together).
  • Flags — White or checkered as its own plaque (same timing as the Dash wrap). Optional yellow (crash ahead) and blue (being lapped). Draws nothing when no flag is up.
  • Systems — CPU and memory in twin columns, process load under each, FPS, ping, and GPU in the footer.
  • Stance — Sit/stand plaque. Follows a bind you set; it does not read rider animation from the game. Details under User requested.

⬇️ Install

  1. Download from https://holeshot-hud.vercel.app/ (or the latest `HoleshotHUD-Setup.exe from download button above`).
  2. If SmartScreen pops up: **More info → Run anyway**. No admin needed.
  3. Setup finds Steam MX Bikes and drops the plugin in. If it can’t, pick the folder with `mxbikes.exe`.
  4. Set MX Bikes to borderless or windowed.
  5. Start the game, then start Holeshot HUD.
  6. Restart MX Bikes after the first plugin install.
Recommended: in Settings, turn on Update automatically on launch. Builds ship constantly, and that setting installs them when you start the overlay.
Optional: Open when MX Bikes opens / Close when MX Bikes closes, so the overlay follows the game.

🙋 User requested

These came from riders in Feedback. Status is as of this post:
  • Stance — ✅ Sit/stand plaque on the overlay (F8 → Stance, turn on Show on overlay). MX Bikes does not send rider posture, so this does not pull sit/stand from the game. It only follows a bind you set: click Sit button, then press a pad, key, or mouse. Default is Right bumper, toggle. Sitting hides the widget unless you turn on Show sitting.
  • Follow a rider in replay — ✅ In replay or spectate, click a rider on Standings or a card on Horizontal Standings to jump the camera to them. Map and minimap treat that rider as you. While you are riding, those clicks go through to the game.
  • Fuel level — ✅ Header and footer option on Standings and Relative, a side slot on Horizontal Standings, and a Dash footer slot. Pick Fuel (liters or gallons from Units) or Fuel %.
  • Lean — ✅ Bike roll, pitch, and steering for the rider the camera is on (F8 → Lean, turn on Show on overlay). Figure or Minimal. This is not sit / stand.
