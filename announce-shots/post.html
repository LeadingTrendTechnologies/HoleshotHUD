Holeshot HUD — a free, fully customizable overlay for MX Bikes
MX Bikes does not give you much on-screen. Holeshot HUD sits on top of the game and shows live race data the way *you* want it: standings, gaps, a dash, telemetry, maps, and who’s around you.
It is fully customizable. Nothing is locked in. Every widget can be turned on or off, moved, resized, restyled, and filled with the data you actually use. Build a tiny race HUD, a full broadcast layout, or anything in between.
Try the widgets in the browser first (demo data), then download for Windows:
https://holeshot-hud.vercel.app/
This is still a work in progress. If something’s off, or you have an idea, there’s a Feedback tab in the app (F8 → Feedback) to report bugs, leave a rating, and send feature ideas. I read those and use them to decide what to fix and build next.

🏁 Why use it

  • See the race, not just the bike. Place, gap (time or laps), interval, best/last lap, who is ahead and behind you, and where everyone is on the track. Header and footer slots can show live gap to the rider one place ahead or behind.
  • See your inputs. Telemetry traces throttle and brake over the last few seconds, with clutch / brake / throttle bars and gear plus speed.
  • Passes update live. Order on the boards follows the track, so you don’t wait on the game classification to catch up.
  • White / checkered flags on the dash or as their own Flags widget, plus a `~Lapped` tag when you’re a lap down.
  • Proximity. Radar with range rings so close vs far is a glance; Relative for the battle you’re actually in.
  • Free. No account. No admin install. Setup finds Steam MX Bikes and drops the plugin in.
  • Try before you install. The site has the same widgets as the app. Turn things on, drag them, change columns and fonts, then download if you like it. Holeshot HUD — live widget editor
  • Fits how you play. Launch with MX Bikes, close with the game, remap the settings key if Medal already uses F8.
  • Replay your line vs the field. **Motos** (F8 → Profile) stores recent races on this PC so you can open Analyze and compare your line, speed, and throttle to another rider at the same spot.
  • See how you race. **Profile** (F8 → Profile → Overview, while Motos recording is on) draws an eight-spoke spider from eligible races. All time or last 14 days. **Tracks** shows your best lap against a living ideal line (Settings → Labs → Experimental features). **Clear** on Profile or Motos wipes that store only, after you confirm.
  • Updated constantly. New builds land often. Enable **Update automatically on launch** in Settings (recommended) so you pick them up when the overlay starts.

📺 Stream

Put your HUD on the stream. Lay the widgets out in the browser. The game window stays yours.
  • One URL. Turn on F8 → Settings → Stream → Browser Source. Add that URL in OBS or Streamlabs as a Browser Source, or in XSplit as a Webpage source. The page is transparent, so only the widgets show. Set the source to the size shown on /edit, then scale it to the canvas.
  • Edit stream. The top bar, or the second URL, opens a page where you drag and resize. Practice, Warmup, Race, and Spectate each keep their own layout.
  • Stream only. That layout does not change the widgets on the game window.
  • Copy. Copy to on /edit copies the layout you are editing onto another session. Copy game → stream copies the in-game layout for the preset open in F8.
  • On track. Widgets show on track and hide in the garage, the same way they do in the game.

⚙️ Fully customizable

A fresh install starts **blank** — every widget is hidden until you turn on **Show on overlay**. You only put on screen what you want.
Then make it yours:
  • Layout — Hold **Ctrl** and drag to move or resize. Snap any widget to a corner, edge, or center of the monitor.
  • Look — Font family (Exo 2, Teko, Goldman, Montserrat, Segoe, Arial, Tahoma, Roboto), per-widget font size, bold, and panel opacity.
  • Units — Metric or imperial for the whole HUD.
  • Columns — Standings and Relative: turn columns on/off, drag to reorder, slide widths. Position, number, name, gap, interval, laps, best/last, last lap diff, bike, status, penalty, and more.
  • Headers and footers — Three slots each on Standings / Relative (session time, riders, track, fuel, fuel %, gap ahead, gap behind, last lap diff, etc.). Dash footer is three slots you pick (temps, laps, gap, interval, gap behind, last lap diff, bike, class, fuel, …). Optional rev bar.
  • Maps — Toggle other riders, start/finish, sector lines (S1 at the line), direction arrows, leader crown, nearest ahead/behind, Follow me, and numbers or position in the dots. Ahead/behind rings stay off in warmup and practice.
  • Horizontal Standings — Broadcast-style bar: how many riders, track name, autoscroll, side info (including last lap diff).
  • Radar — Look is Plaque (default) or Arrows. Side and/or rear proximity, with optional 3 / 6 / 12 m range rings on Plaque.
Press F8 for the overlay menu (or pick another key): Widgets, Profile, Settings, and Feedback. Profile has Overview, Motos, and Tracks. Layout is saved. Want a different look next session? Change it in a few clicks — you are not stuck with a default HUD.

🧩 Widgets

  • Standings — Who is ahead and by how much. Place, number, name, gap (time or laps), best/last lap, fuel, and more. Status shows finish, crash, DNS, OUT, DSQ, and pit icons. Optional Last lap diff (last lap minus the one before: faster green, slower red, same 0.000) stays off until you turn it on, as a column or a header/footer slot. Header and footer can also show live gap to the rider one place ahead or behind. Your row is highlighted. In replay, click a name to follow that rider.
  • Relative — Riders just ahead and behind you, so you can see the battle you’re actually in without scanning the full board. Fuel, gap ahead, gap behind, and last lap diff can sit in the header or footer (those gaps use race place, not the riders in the table). Last lap diff is that rider’s own previous lap, and it stays off until you turn it on.
  • Horizontal Standings — Broadcast-style bar: leader on the left, your name highlighted. Optional track name, autoscroll, and fuel / gap ahead / gap behind / last lap diff in a side slot. Card gaps stay vs you. Last lap diff stays off until you turn it on. In replay, click a card to follow that rider.
  • Map — Full track outline with you and other riders, start/finish, optional S1/S2/S3 sector lines (S1 at the line), direction arrows, leader crown, and optional numbers or position in the dots. Follow me (off by default) keeps you centered, nose up, and rotates the track around you; north stays the direction you are traveling. Ahead/behind rings stay off in warmup and practice. Blue if they’re a lap ahead and closing, red if you’re a lap up and closing.
  • Minimap — Zoomed circular view of the track around you, same rider markers and sector lines. The outline is smoothed. Ahead/behind rings match Map: off in warmup and practice.
  • Radar — Who is beside and behind you. Look is Plaque (range rings and blips, default) or Arrows (larger tips on the screen edge that slide with the bike). A nearby crash flashes on the arrow for about 1.75 s, then stays hidden. Plaque still shows downed riders.
  • Dash — Gear, speed, RPM, position, and a footer you can set (including fuel, gap, interval, gap behind, and last lap diff). Last lap diff stays off until you turn it on. Optional rev bar, or Simple dash (gear and speed only). Optional Shift color flushes the gear faint red at the shift light or limiter (off by default). Session clock and race flags (white / checkered) sit with the dash.
  • Telemetry — Throttle (green) and brake (red) traces, clutch / brake / throttle bars, and a gear/speed dial with an orange RPM arc. Turn sections or channels off; hidden parts reflow. Optional steer is off until you turn it on. Riding uses your bike inputs. Spectate follows that rider’s throttle and front brake. Restart MX Bikes after install so the plugin can publish those inputs.
  • Lean — Bike roll, pitch, and steering for the rider the camera is on. Figure (rear-view rider) or Minimal (numbers). Not sit / stand.
  • Controller — Live DualShock or Xbox under Cockpit. Turn it on with Show on overlay. Theme is Light or Dark. Sticks follow your hands. Triggers fill with squeeze. Bumpers light when held.
  • Delta Bar — Time vs your best at this point on the lap, per class on that track. Not the in-game ghost.
  • Sectors — Split times (S1–S3) and live delta vs your best at this point in the sector. Best times are per class on that track (all 250s together, all 450s together).
  • Flags — White or checkered as its own plaque (same timing as the Dash wrap). Optional yellow (crash ahead) and blue (being lapped). Draws nothing when no flag is up.
  • Systems — CPU and memory in twin columns, process load under each, FPS, ping, and GPU in the footer.
  • Stance — Sit/stand plaque. Follows a bind you set; it does not read rider animation from the game. Details under User requested.

🏍️ Motos

Under F8 → Profile → Motos. Recording starts off until you Enable on the Motos page. The empty page shows what Analyze looks like, then Enable. Turn Record off anytime to stop saving new motos; ones already on the list stay until you delete them.
  • List — Recent races on this PC as a timing sheet (when, track, riders, you, fastest). Chips are All / Ranked / Saved. Trash and bookmark icons (Delete / Save / Saved). A race win shows a gold crown. Record switch in the heading. Clear on the far right wipes stored motos after you confirm (Profile stays; a live moto is kept).
  • Analyze — Map beside SPEED / HEIGHT / throttle tapes. Zoom a corner, scrub the lap, pick Compare to read another rider at the same spot. At this spot under the map; Results below. Motos last about two weeks unless you Save.
  • Live — During a moto, F8 → Profile → Motos opens that visit while recording is on (new lines show on the next paint).

📈 Profile

Only while Motos Record is on. Your in-game name sits over an eight-spoke spider of race traits from real races with a field (practice, warmup, and solo do not count).
  • Axes — Pace, Smooth, Clean, Clutch, Attack, Air, Results, Opening. Hover a spoke for what it scores. Fill uses your Settings accent.
  • Windows — All time or last 14 days. Compact scores stay after Motos drops old Analyze tapes. Banner until five races. Holeshot count sits under the moto line.
  • Tracks — Best Motos lap per track, plus a map of that lap against a living ideal line. Behind Settings → Labs → Experimental features.
  • Clear — Far right on Profile and the Motos list. Confirm first. Profile wipe leaves Motos; Motos wipe leaves Profile (a live moto stays).

⬇️ Install

  1. Download from https://holeshot-hud.vercel.app/ (or the latest `HoleshotHUD-Setup.exe from download button above`).
  2. If SmartScreen pops up: **More info → Run anyway**. No admin needed.
  3. Setup finds Steam MX Bikes and drops the plugin in. If it can’t, pick the folder with `mxbikes.exe`.
  4. Set MX Bikes to borderless or windowed.
  5. Start the game, then start Holeshot HUD.
  6. Restart MX Bikes after the first plugin install.
Recommended: in Settings, turn on Update automatically on launch. Builds ship constantly, and that setting installs them when you start the overlay.
Optional: Open when MX Bikes opens / Close when MX Bikes closes, so the overlay follows the game.

🙋 User requested

These came from riders in Feedback. Status is as of this post:
  • Stance — ✅ Sit/stand plaque on the overlay (F8 → Stance, turn on Show on overlay). MX Bikes does not send rider posture, so this does not pull sit/stand from the game. It only follows a bind you set: click Sit button, then press a pad, key, or mouse. Default is Right bumper, toggle. Sitting hides the widget unless you turn on Show sitting.
  • Follow a rider in replay — ✅ In replay or spectate, click a rider on Standings or a card on Horizontal Standings to jump the camera to them. Map and minimap treat that rider as you. While you are riding, those clicks go through to the game.
  • Fuel level — ✅ Header and footer option on Standings and Relative, a side slot on Horizontal Standings, and a Dash footer slot. Pick Fuel (liters or gallons from Units) or Fuel %.
